================================================================================
GEMINI LEAD SCORING LOGIC
Aangan Studio - hot / warm / cold classification of qualified transcripts
Companion to: Vaani qualification rubric
As of 9 October 2026
================================================================================

WHAT THIS IS

Vaani answers the call and decides whether Aangan can serve the caller at all.
Gemini reads the transcript of the calls Vaani passed and decides how much of a
designer's attention the lead deserves and how fast.

These are different questions. Vaani asks "can we do this work?" Gemini asks
"how close is this person to signing, and how large is the project?" A lead can
pass every one of Vaani's five gates and still be someone who will decide in six
months.

Gemini never re-runs qualification. It receives only transcripts Vaani marked
QUALIFIED, and it classifies them.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INPUT CONTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Gemini receives, per lead:

  transcript_id      e.g. "T05"
  transcript         full text of the call
  channel            "phone"
  received_at        ISO timestamp, including out-of-hours calls
  gate_results       Vaani's five gate verdicts, each pass / pass-with-note /
                     unresolved
  open_questions     anything Vaani could not resolve in two questions
  recovery_flag      true if the caller had enquired before and got no reply

Gemini receives nothing else. It has no CRM history, no past project list, and
no designer calendar. It must not behave as though it does.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TWO AXES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"Hot" is not one property. In the September transcripts two different things
drive it, and they move independently:

  READINESS  -  how close this person is to committing
  VALUE      -  how large the project is in designer-days and revenue

T15 (Undri 2BHK, possession in six weeks, husband already said go ahead) is
high readiness and modest value. T12 (Kalyani Nagar villa, 5,500 sq ft) is high
value and was, until he asked for a site visit this week, moderate readiness.
Collapsing both into one number before scoring loses the reason for the answer,
and the reason is what the designer needs.

Score both. Then band.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AXIS A - READINESS (0 to 10, floor at 0, cap at 10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

POSITIVE SIGNALS

  +3   NAMED REFERRER WHO IS A PAST CLIENT OR KNOWN TO THE STUDIO
       The caller names a person, and that person is described as having had
       work done by Aangan or as knowing the studio.
       Evidence in the pack: T01 (friend had her flat done in Aundh), T05
       (friend of the founder), W01 (referrer's flat done last year), W06
       (referrer told them not to look further), F05 (their CEO worked with
       Aangan before).
       An unnamed referral ("a friend recommended you") scores +1, not +3.
       Instagram, Google, LinkedIn and press score 0. They are not negative.

  +2   SITE IS EMPTY OR THE CALLER HAS VACATED
       New possession, bare shell, builder handover, or "we have already moved
       out". Execution can start without choreography.
       Evidence: T05, T06, T12, W01, F09.

  +2   DECISION IS ALREADY MADE BETWEEN THE DECIDERS
       The caller reports that the other decision-maker has agreed, or that
       both will attend the consultation.
       Evidence: T01, T15 ("he said to go ahead and book"), T20 (both want to
       be at the consultation), F01.
       This is distinct from Vaani's gate 5. Gate 5 asks whether the decider
       will be present; this asks whether they have already said yes.

  +2   A SPECIFIC START OR COMPLETION MONTH IS NAMED
       "By March", "operational by December", "January start".
       A season or a vague horizon ("soon", "in a few months") scores +1.

  +2   SITE VISIT OR START REQUESTED WITHIN TWO WEEKS
       Evidence: T12 ("this week or next"), T15 (can access the site now),
       F05 ("speed matters", "ready to move fast").

  +1   THE CALLER ASKED TO BOOK, OR ACCEPTED A SLOT ON THE CALL
       Almost every forwarded lead scores this. It is a weak signal precisely
       because it is near-universal.

  +1   VOLUNTEERED A BUDGET THAT COMFORTABLY FITS THE SCOPE DESCRIBED
       Volunteering a workable figure unprompted signals they have thought it
       through. See the hard rule on pricing below - the figure is never
       restated as a quote and never compared to Aangan's rate bands in output.

NEGATIVE SIGNALS

  -2   EXPLICITLY COMPARING STUDIOS AND UNHURRIED
       Both conditions together. Comparing alone is normal and scores 0.
       Evidence: F06 ("shortlisted 3 studios ... not in a rush to decide").

  -3   DEFERRED THE DECISION ON THE CALL
       "Let me think and call back", "maybe later", "we're still early stage".
       Evidence: T07, W10.

  -1   CALLER IS AN INTERMEDIARY AND THE DECIDER HAS NOT YET ENGAGED
       The lead still passed gate 5, so the penalty is small.
       Evidence: T14 ("I'm just calling to see if it's worth pursuing").

  -1   SITE ACCESS IS MORE THAN THREE MONTHS AWAY WITH NO DESIGN START AGREED
       Long lead time is not disqualifying - Aangan's design phase is 3-4 weeks
       and can run ahead - but it delays revenue and lets a competitor in.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AXIS B - VALUE (0 to 10, cap at 10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This estimates designer-days and project size. It is NEVER a price, and nothing
from this axis is spoken to a caller or written as a figure in the brief.

  CARPET AREA                    2,000 sq ft or more        +4
                                 1,000 to 1,999 sq ft       +3
                                 600 to 999 sq ft           +2
                                 under 600 sq ft            +1
                                 not stated                 +2  (neutral default)

  SCOPE                          full home or full fitout   +3
                                 two or more rooms          +2
                                 single room                +1

  SPECIFICATION MARKERS          +1 each, maximum +2
                                 - villa or independent house
                                 - principal or senior designer requested
                                 - caller leads with materials, finishes or a
                                   named aesthetic
                                 - caller moved out specifically for execution

  COMMERCIAL HEADCOUNT           50 or more workstations    +2
                                 20 to 49 workstations      +1
                                 (replaces specification markers for offices)

The "not stated" default of +2 is deliberate. Absence of a carpet area is not
evidence of a small project - it is evidence that nobody asked. T13 and T16 both
reached booking without area being established.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BANDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  TOTAL = READINESS + VALUE          (0 to 20)

  HOT     total 13 or more, OR readiness 8 or more at any value
  WARM    total 7 to 12
  COLD    total 6 or less

The readiness override exists because a decided buyer with a small flat is worth
answering before an undecided buyer with a large one. Readiness converts; value
only determines how much the conversion is worth.

OVERRIDES, APPLIED AFTER BANDING

  RECOVERY   recovery_flag is true (T16, W08 pattern). Route as HOT regardless
             of band. This caller is deciding whether to trust the studio, and
             the cost of a second failure is the relationship, not the project.

  THIN       fewer than three scored signals in total. Band is advisory only;
             set confidence to "low" and put the missing facts in
             open_questions. Do not let a thin transcript produce a confident
             COLD.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE BANDS MEAN OPERATIONALLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  HOT     Senior designer. Offer a site visit, not a phone consultation.
          Brief sent immediately; first human contact within the hour, whatever
          time the call came in.

  WARM    Next available designer. Phone or studio consultation.
          Brief sent within the hour; contact same working day.

  COLD    Standard queue. Phone consultation. Brief sent with the day's batch.

COLD DOES NOT MEAN IGNORE. Every lead Gemini sees has already passed all five of
Vaani's gates, which means Aangan can serve them and wants to. The band decides
who calls and how fast, never whether anyone calls. An enquiry answered within
an hour converts at four times the rate of one answered the next day, and that
multiplier applies to cold leads too.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HARD RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. NEVER RE-QUALIFY.
   Gemini does not overturn Vaani's gates. If the transcript suggests a gate was
   wrong - the site is actually in Talegaon, the caller actually wants advice
   only - Gemini sets band to COLD, confidence to "low", and writes the conflict
   into open_questions for a human. It does not reclassify the lead as
   unqualified.

2. NEVER OUTPUT A PRICE.
   No figure, no range, no per-square-foot rate, no estimate of what the project
   will cost. A budget the caller volunteered is recorded as a signal and may be
   passed through verbatim to the designer's brief, but Gemini never evaluates
   it against Aangan's rate bands in anything a caller could see, and never
   generates a number of its own.

3. EVERY SIGNAL NEEDS A QUOTE.
   A signal scores only if Gemini can cite a fragment of the transcript, twelve
   words or fewer, that supports it. No quote, no points. This is what makes the
   score auditable when a designer disagrees with it.

4. ABSENCE IS NOT A PENALTY.
   An unstated fact scores zero and goes into not_established. It never
   subtracts. The negative signals above all require the caller to have actively
   said something.

5. NEVER SCORE ON LOCALITY, NAME, LANGUAGE OR ACCENT.
   Do not infer budget or seriousness from which part of Pune the site is in.
   Do not score a Hindi or Marathi call differently from an English one - W03
   was conducted entirely in Hindi and converted. Area and scope are scored
   because the caller stated them; a postcode is not a signal.

6. NEVER INVENT.
   No facts that are not in the transcript. No inferred carpet areas, no assumed
   timelines, no guessed relationships to past clients.

7. ESCALATIONS NEVER REACH GEMINI.
   An existing-project complaint (T09 pattern) is routed to a human by Vaani
   before this step. If one arrives here anyway, output band "ESCALATE" and stop
   scoring.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OUTPUT FORMAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Return strict JSON. No prose outside it, no markdown fences.

{
  "transcript_id": "T05",
  "band": "HOT",
  "total": 15,
  "readiness": {
    "score": 8,
    "signals": [
      {"signal": "named_referrer_known_to_studio", "weight": 3,
       "evidence": "Vikram Agarwal asked me to call"},
      {"signal": "site_vacated", "weight": 2,
       "evidence": "We've already moved out temporarily"},
      {"signal": "specific_month_named", "weight": 2,
       "evidence": "move back in by February"},
      {"signal": "booking_accepted", "weight": 1,
       "evidence": "Yes please - Vikram said your team does that"}
    ]
  },
  "value": {
    "score": 7,
    "signals": [
      {"signal": "carpet_area_2000_plus", "weight": 4,
       "evidence": "about 2,400 sq ft carpet"},
      {"signal": "full_home", "weight": 3,
       "evidence": "Complete redesign. New flooring, kitchen"}
    ]
  },
  "overrides_applied": [],
  "routing": "senior_designer_site_visit_within_1h",
  "confidence": "high",
  "not_established": ["current_state_of_flooring"],
  "open_questions": [],
  "one_line_for_designer": "Referred by a friend of Nikhil's, flat already vacated, February deadline."
}

FIELD RULES

  band                  HOT | WARM | COLD | ESCALATE
  total                 must equal readiness.score + value.score
  confidence            "high"   both carpet area and timeline established
                        "medium" one of the two missing
                        "low"    both missing, or fewer than three signals, or
                                 a gate conflict was detected
  one_line_for_designer at most 20 words, facts only, no band label, no number.
                        This is the line that appears at the top of the
                        Telegram brief.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN WEAKNESSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - The weights are fitted to twenty phone calls from one month, and none of
    them has a known outcome. Nobody knows which of these twelve actually
    signed. Until booked-versus-signed data exists, these numbers encode
    judgement, not evidence.

  - "Named referrer who is a past client" at +3 is the single heaviest signal
    and rests on five transcripts. If it is wrong, the whole top of the
    distribution is wrong.

  - Value is a proxy for a proxy. Carpet area stands in for project size, which
    stands in for revenue, and specification is the part that actually moves the
    number - which Gemini cannot see from a four-minute call.

  - A caller who is simply articulate will score higher than one who is not,
    because every signal requires them to have said something. Rule 4 limits the
    damage but does not remove it.

  - There is no feedback loop yet. Re-score 20 live calls each month against
    what actually converted, and move the weights deliberately rather than
    letting them drift.
